How To Build a Personal AI Workflow in Under 30 Minutes

📅 March 24, 2026⏱ 7 min read✍️ AI Tools Hub

Most people use AI tools reactively — opening ChatGPT when they remember it exists. The people getting 10x more done have built intentional workflows. Here's how to do it in 30 minutes.

Step 1: Pick Your Core AI (5 min)

Choose one primary AI assistant and stick with it. Don't jump between tools for the same task. Our recommendation: Claude for most people — excellent writing, huge context window, honest about uncertainty.

Step 2: Set Up Research (5 min)

Bookmark Perplexity AI and use it every time you'd normally Google something. It gives you sourced answers instead of a list of links. Train yourself to reach for it first.

Step 3: Automate Repetitive Tasks (10 min)

Sign up for Zapier free tier. Connect your email to AI summarization. Set up one automation that saves you at least 30 minutes per week. Start small — one working automation beats ten planned ones.

Step 4: Build Prompt Templates (10 min)

Create a simple notes file with your 5 most-used prompts. Things like: "Summarize this in 3 bullet points", "Write a professional email responding to this", "Review this code for bugs". Copy-paste beats rewriting every time.

The Stack

  • Claude — primary thinking/writing partner
  • Perplexity — research and fact-checking
  • Zapier — background automation

That's it. Three tools, all with free tiers, covering 90% of what most people need from AI.
